1. Introduction

1.1 About the System

The VETA Hotel Booking System is a web-based application designed to streamline hotel operations. It allows guests to browse rooms, make bookings online, and receive a unique reference ID for their reservation. Hotel staff can manage rooms, process bookings, handle payments, and track guest check-ins and check-outs from a centralized dashboard.

1.2 Who Should Use This Manual

  • Hotel Guests: Anyone who wants to browse rooms and make a booking.
  • Front Desk Staff: Staff who process check-ins, check-outs, and payments.
  • Hotel Managers: Administrators who manage rooms and staff accounts.

1.3 System Requirements

  • A computer, tablet, or smartphone with internet access
  • A modern web browser (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Safari)
  • JavaScript enabled in your browser

1.4 Accessing the System

Open your web browser and navigate to the system URL provided by your hotel administrator. The homepage will display with options to browse rooms, register, or log in.

📌 Note: Staff accounts are created by the system administrator. If you are a staff member, you will receive your login credentials via email.

2. Guest (Customer) Guide

2.1 Creating an Account

To make a booking, you need to create a guest account:

1

Click "Register"

On the homepage, click the Register Now button or navigate to the Register page.

2

Fill in Your Details

Enter your first name, last name, email address, phone number (e.g., +255712345678), and password. Address is optional.

3

Submit

Agree to the terms and conditions and click Create Account. You will be automatically logged in.

💡 Tip: Use a valid phone number. You'll need it when checking in at the hotel.

2.2 Logging In

1

Go to Login Page

Click the Login link in the navigation menu.

2

Enter Credentials

Type your email address or phone number and your password.

3

Sign In

Click Sign In. You will be redirected to the homepage.

2.3 Browsing Available Rooms

You can browse rooms with or without an account:

1

Navigate to Rooms

Click Browse Rooms from the homepage or navigation menu.

2

Use Search Filters (Optional)

Select your Check-in and Check-out dates, choose a room type, or set a maximum price to filter results.

3

View Room Details

Click View Details on any room card to see full information including images, features, and pricing.

2.4 Making a Booking

1

Select a Room

From the room list or room detail page, click Book Now (you must be logged in).

2

Fill Booking Form

Confirm or change your check-in date, check-out date, and number of guests. Add any special requests if needed.

3

Confirm Booking

Review the price summary and click Confirm Booking.

4

Save Your Reference ID

You will receive a unique Reference ID (e.g., VETA-2026-0042). Save this number — you'll need it when you arrive at the hotel.

⚠️ Important: Your booking is initially marked as Pending. The hotel staff will approve it. Payment is processed at the hotel when you arrive.

2.5 Understanding Your Reference ID

Your booking reference ID follows this format:

VETA-2026-0042
Part Meaning Example
VETA Hotel identifier VETA Hotel
2026 Year of booking 2026
0042 Unique sequence number 42nd booking of the year
📌 Remember: You can provide either this reference ID OR your registered phone number when you arrive at the hotel.

2.6 Viewing & Managing Bookings

1

Go to My Bookings

Click My Bookings from the navigation menu (you must be logged in).

2

Filter by Status (Optional)

Use the filter tabs at the top to show only Pending, Approved, Checked In, or other statuses.

3

View Details

Click View Details on any booking to see the full timeline and status flow.

2.7 Cancelling a Booking

You can cancel a booking as long as it hasn't been checked in yet:

1

Open the Booking

Go to My Bookings and find the booking you want to cancel.

2

Click Cancel

Click the Cancel button (only visible for bookings that can be cancelled).

3

Provide Reason

Type a brief reason for cancellation and click Yes, Cancel Booking.

⚠️ Note: Bookings that are already Checked In cannot be cancelled online.

2.8 Checking In at the Hotel

When you arrive at VETA Hotel, follow these steps:

1

Visit Reception

Go to the front desk with your identification.

2

Provide Booking Information

Give the staff your Reference ID (e.g., VETA-2026-0042) or your registered phone number.

3

Make Payment

Pay the booking amount at the reception. The staff will update your payment status in the system.

4

Get Your Room Key

Once payment is confirmed, the staff will check you in and provide your room key.

Check-in time: 2:00 PM | Check-out time: 11:00 AM

3. Staff (Administrator) Guide

3.1 Staff Login

1

Access Staff Login

On the guest login page, click the "Staff member?" link at the bottom. Alternatively, navigate to /account/staff-login/.

2

Enter Credentials

Type your staff email and password provided by your administrator.

3

Access Dashboard

After successful login, you will be redirected to the staff dashboard.

📌 Note: Staff accounts are created by the system administrator. Contact your manager if you need an account.

3.2 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides a live overview of hotel operations:

Section What It Shows
Available Rooms Number of rooms currently available for booking
Occupied Rooms Number of rooms currently occupied by guests
Checked In Today Guests who checked in today
Today's Revenue Total payments received today (TZS)
Pending Bookings New bookings awaiting approval
Currently In-house List of guests currently checked in
Upcoming Check-ins Guests expected to arrive tomorrow
Quick Search Find a guest by phone number or reference ID

3.3 Managing Rooms

From the sidebar, click Manage Rooms to view all rooms.

Adding a New Room

1

Click "Add New Room"

On the Rooms page, click the Add New Room button.

2

Fill Room Details

Enter the room number, select room type, set floor, price per night (TZS), and capacity.

3

Upload Image (Optional)

Add a photo of the room (recommended for better guest experience).

4

Set Status & Save

Set the room status to Available and click Add Room.

Updating Room Status

Status When to Use
Available Room is ready for booking
Occupied Guest is currently staying in the room (auto-set during check-in)
Maintenance Room is under repair or cleaning (not bookable)
Reserved Room is held for an approved booking (auto-set during approval)

3.4 Managing Bookings

From the sidebar, click All Bookings to view and manage all bookings.

You can filter bookings by:

  • Search: By reference ID, guest name, or phone number
  • Status: Pending, Approved, Checked In, Checked Out, Cancelled
  • Date Range: Filter by check-in/check-out dates

Approving a Booking

1

Open the Booking

Click on a booking from the list to view its details.

2

Click "Approve Booking"

In the Quick Actions section, click the Approve Booking button.

3

Status Changes

The booking status changes to Approved and the room is marked as Reserved.

Booking Status Flow

Pending → Approved → Paid → Checked In → Checked Out

(Cancellation is possible at Pending or Approved stages)

3.5 Processing Payments

Payments are processed when the guest arrives at the hotel:

1

Find the Booking

Search for the guest using their Reference ID or phone number.

2

Open Booking Details

Click on the booking to view full details including the total amount.

3

Collect Payment

Receive payment from the guest (cash, card, mobile money, etc.).

4

Mark as Paid

Click the Mark as Paid button. The payment status changes to Paid.

⚠️ Important: The Check In option only becomes available after the payment has been marked as Paid.

3.6 Guest Check-In Process

1

Verify Payment

Ensure the booking payment status is Paid.

2

Click "Check In Guest"

In the Quick Actions section, click Check In Guest.

3

Confirm

The booking status changes to Checked In and the room status becomes Occupied.

4

Give Room Key

Provide the guest with their room key and direct them to their room.

3.7 Guest Check-Out Process

1

Find the Guest

Search for the guest or find them in the Currently In-house list on the dashboard.

2

Open Booking

Click on the booking to view details.

3

Click "Check Out Guest"

Click the Check Out Guest button (warm/golden color).

4

Confirmation

The booking status changes to Checked Out and the room becomes Available again.

5

Collect Key

Collect the room key from the guest before they leave.

3.8 Searching for Guests

You can search for guests in two ways:

Method 1: Quick Search (Dashboard)

Use the search box at the bottom of the dashboard page. Enter a phone number or reference ID.

Method 2: Advanced Search

Click Search Guest in the sidebar. Enter a phone number or reference ID and click Search. The system will display the guest's information and all their bookings.

3.9 Adding New Staff Members

Only administrators can add new staff accounts:

1

Navigate to Staff List

From the sidebar, click Staff List (available to admins only).

2

Click "Add Staff"

Click the Add Staff button at the top.

3

Enter Staff Details

Fill in the staff member's name, email, phone, position, and set a password.

4

Create Account

Click Create Staff Account. The staff member can now log in using their email and the password you set.

💡 Best Practice: Create individual accounts for each staff member. Set unique passwords and ask staff to change them on first login. Deactivate staff accounts when employees leave.

4.4 Troubleshooting Common Issues

Issue: "Cannot check in guest"

Solution: Ensure the booking is Approved AND has Paid payment status. The Check In button only appears when both conditions are met.

Issue: "Room is not available for selected dates"

Solution: The room is already booked for these dates. Try different dates or select a different room. The system prevents double-booking automatically.

Issue: "Cannot find a guest"

Solution: Try searching with the guest's phone number instead of the reference ID, or vice versa. Ensure the number matches what the guest used during registration.

Issue: "Forgot password"

Solution: Staff members should contact the system administrator to reset their password. Guest users should contact hotel reception for assistance.

Issue: "Page not loading properly"

Solution: Clear your browser cache and cookies, then refresh the page. Ensure you have a stable internet connection. If the problem persists, try using a different browser (Chrome is recommended).

Issue: "Room image not displaying"

Solution: This is usually a temporary loading issue. Refresh the page. If the problem continues, the image file may not have been uploaded correctly. Re-upload the room image from the Staff Room Management page.
